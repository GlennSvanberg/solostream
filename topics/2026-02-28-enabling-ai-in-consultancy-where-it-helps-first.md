# Narrative brief

A two-host deep dive on enabling AI in a consultancy as a change-management and craft problem, not a procurement problem. Structure: (1) Map consultancy work into stages—discovery, analysis, synthesis, deliverables, stakeholder management—and identify high-leverage, low-risk AI assists (meeting notes, research summaries, first-draft slides, proposal variants, code/data accelerators). (2) Discuss governance and quality: review processes, human accountability, client confidentiality, data boundaries, and how to prevent “hallucinated authority” in client-facing work. (3) Adoption realities: incentives, training by project role (partners vs analysts), internal prompt/playbook library, and creating safe sandboxes. (4) Business implications: pricing models, differentiation, and how AI shifts what clients value (judgment, framing, relationships). Conclude with a phased rollout plan: pilot teams, metrics (cycle time, rework rate), and a feedback loop that upgrades practice without burning trust.
