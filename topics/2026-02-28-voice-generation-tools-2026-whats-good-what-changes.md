# Narrative brief

Host interviews a guest about the current voice generation landscape from a practical user perspective. Cover: what “good” means (natural prosody, controllability, multilingual, emotions, consistency across episodes, pronunciation tools, licensing). Walk through a few categories: text-to-speech, voice cloning, conversational voice, and production helpers (cleanup, mastering). Ask about workflow: script → voice → edits → publishing, plus where time is really spent. Include ethical and trust considerations without moralizing: disclosure, consent, watermarking, and how audiences perceive synthetic voices. Close with a short checklist listeners can use to choose a tool for their needs (budget, latency, quality, rights).
