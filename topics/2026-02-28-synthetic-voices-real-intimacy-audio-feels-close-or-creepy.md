# Narrative brief

A brief, thoughtful solo observation on the emotional texture of voice. Explore one idea: intimacy in audio comes less from perfect realism and more from timing, breath, and small imperfections that signal attention. Contrast ‘polished’ narration with ‘present’ narration, and suggest a listener-centric principle for using AI voice responsibly: optimize for clarity and honesty, not imitation. End with a question the audience can carry: ‘Does this voice sound like it’s trying to be someone, or like it’s trying to say something?’
